## The path so far

### Multi-tenant Fabric product

I work remotely from Munich as a contributor on the product team behind a multi-tenant analytics product on Microsoft Fabric. One large semantic model serves many customers, each with their own Dev, Test and Prod workspaces, all running on shared Fabric capacity. On shared capacity a slow measure is everyone's problem, so the work is about scale: DAX measures that stay fast as the model grows, data pipelines and APIs that feed it, and data apps built on top. Most of it I build agentically, with coding agents working on the model's source files and Git behind every change.

### Data products in fintech

Before that I built data products inside a fintech in Munich, end to end: gathering the requirements, building the warehouse, and putting Power BI dashboards on top. Postgres and dbt underneath, Power BI on Fabric and Azure, and Excel wherever finance needed it. The other half of the job was finance itself: revenue forecasting, scenario planning, KPI reporting, financial modelling and M&amp;A due diligence, plus the stakeholder work of agreeing what a number means before anyone builds it.

### Graduate studies in Quantitative Economics

At Christian-Albrechts University Kiel, focused on time series econometrics and foreign exchange markets. I left the programme for work in Munich. The interest in FX stayed: it is what the FX Macro Data Platform is built around.

### Insurance and real estate

Work in insurance and real estate in Berlin, between the two degrees.

### Bachelor's in Business Administration

At Technical University Brandenburg. My thesis was in multivariate macroeconometric forecasting: which macroeconomic aggregates hold up as forecasters of inflation against ARMA and VARMA benchmarks, as a way into how monetary policy reaches prices. That is where the time series started.

### Audio engineering and music production

Before university I was deep into audio engineering and digital music production in Ableton Live. That is where signal processing started for me: sampling, filters, EQ, compression. It came back later in time series econometrics, where a moving average is a low-pass filter and a business cycle is a frequency band. Same mathematics, different signal.

### Abitur at a German school abroad

### School, with a focus on art

I grew up in Berlin and went to the Kurt-Schwitters-Oberschule, where my focus was art, until the move to Moscow.

## How I work

- **Clarity before code.** Architecture follows from a clear picture of what the product is for, not the other way around.

- **Craft in the details.** Correct isn't enough. Naming, layout, load times and edge cases decide whether people come back.

- **Impact you can see.** A dashboard nobody opens is a failed project, however elegant the model.
