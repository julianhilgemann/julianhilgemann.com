### Liquiplan

A planner for fixed-term deposits that answers one question: how long will the money last, and which deposits, bought when, make it last longest?

Money that has to cover a monthly need is caught between two mistakes. Left in an overnight account it earns little; locked into fixed terms it may not be back when the month's withdrawal is due. A deposit ladder staggers the maturities so that each deposit returns just before its money is needed. Liquiplan computes that ladder from the balances, the monthly need and the offers at hand, and it takes its goals strictly in order: first the last month that can still be covered, then the wealth left at that month. Interest never costs a month.

- The horizon first, interest second

- An independent simulator checks every plan

- One HTML file, and the numbers stay in the browser

- Every decision can be replayed step by step

Drawn from the app's own example: six deposits at illustrative rates. A model calculation, not financial advice.
