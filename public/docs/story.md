# From signal to number, and back.

This page follows one signal from the world into a database, down to a single number, and back out as sound.

Everything I do with data is either compression or expansion. Compression to navigate, expansion to express. I build every stage of that chain, and the number in the middle is the narrowest point of it.

It starts as signal.

Signal

## Reality has no sample rate.

Light, sound, pressure, faces: the world reaches you all at once, in continuous time, at a bandwidth nothing could store. It is denser than any instrument, any database and any brain can hold.

Noise is just signal you haven't modelled yet.

→

Belief

## We don't see the world. We see our best current guess about it.

Perception is inference: a prior, updated by evidence, never the thing itself. Your brain doesn't record the signal. It guesses, and keeps correcting the guess.

Resolution: the image sharpens, the decision lands.

→

Measurement

## To measure is to decide what to lose.

Every measurement picks a rate, a resolution and a blind spot. Data starts when someone decides what to write down — and, with it, what not to.

Every measure is a choice.
